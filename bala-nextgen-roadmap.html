01

Do not replace GameChanger.

In select softball, GameChanger is already the default scoring, streaming, video, recap, and stats platform. NextGen should sit around it as the branded public home for the team.

Answers for Bala

Four questions. One clear product lane.

01 · Target customers

Who are the target customers?

The buyer is the team, coach, club, or parent-run organization. The users are parents, family members, players, sponsors, and fans. Game goers matter, but the stronger customer is the team that wants one polished digital home.

02 · Business model

What is the business model?

Start as team-site SaaS. Charge monthly or annually for a branded site with roster, schedule, player profiles, sponsor slots, media, fundraising, and GameChanger links. Add premium RallyIQ features for AI recaps, player cards, recruiting pages, and sponsor analytics.

03 · Game/player data

How are we getting the data?

Assume GameChanger is the source of truth for live scoring, video, and stats. MVP should use manual entry, CSV/stat imports, and deep links to GameChanger games. An unofficial API can be explored for internal prototypes, but should not be the product’s contractual foundation.

04 · Multi-tenant sites

Does each customer get their own site?

Yes. Every team gets its own branded site and domain experience, while the backend stays multi-tenant. Examples: texasvenom.site, atxprimetime.site, teamname.nextgen.team, and custom domains later.

GameChanger is the field. NextGen is the front door, player showcase, sponsor layer, and story engine.

Product roadmap

Build the branded layer first. Add intelligence second.

1

NextGen Team Site MVP

Beautiful team website using the existing NextGen UI: roster, schedule, player profiles, sponsors, photos, announcements, fundraising, and links to GameChanger.

2

Team Admin OS

Simple dashboard for coaches or parent admins to manage content once and publish it everywhere.

3

Game Data Layer

Import stats, link games, attach media, and enrich player pages without trying to beat GameChanger at scoring or streaming.

4

RallyIQ

AI-generated recaps, player summaries, weekly team stories, recruiting cards, highlight prompts, and sponsor performance reports.

5

Rally OS

Expand from one team to clubs, tournaments, multi-team organizations, fan engagement, sponsor inventory, and content automation.

Pitch desk note

How to say it to Bala

“We are not competing head-on with GameChanger. GameChanger is already where the game gets scored and streamed. NextGen is the branded team site and RallyIQ is the intelligence layer that turns existing game activity into player visibility, sponsor value, and automated team storytelling.”
